# Age of Tracks: How Old Is the Trail

## Objective
To determine the age of a track by observing its degradation, which converts tracking from simple observation into prediction, allowing the user to decide whether to invest time and energy in pursuit.

## Dependencies and prerequisites
*   Systematic observation skills.
*   Knowledge of environmental factors (Sun, wind, rain, temperature, humidity).
*   Discipline for the calibration technique.

## Material
*   Tracks in various substrates: Mud, Snow, Sand, and Forest Floor (Leaf Litter).
*   The user's own control print.
*   Environmental conditions (weather).

## System
The system converts tracking from observation into prediction by estimating the age of a track. This estimation answers critical questions: Is the animal still nearby? Is this route actively used? When should a trap or ambush be set?

## Technology
*   **Calibration Technique:** The most reliable method involving comparing the state of an animal track to a control print made in the same substrate.
*   **Degradation Physics:** The rate of decay is determined by environmental factors.

## Tools
*   No specialized equipment is required for the calibration technique; only discipline.
*   Observation and comparison skills.
