# Leafy Greens Foraging Guide

## Objective
To teach the reader to identify, use, and understand the critical survival value of Dandelion, Stinging Nettle, and Broadleaf Plantain as the three most important wild greens for temperate survival.

## Dependencies and prerequisites
*   **Water Intake:** Dandelion is a diuretic, requiring compensation with extra water intake; do not rely heavily on dandelion greens if water is scarce.
*   **Protection:** Stinging nettles must be handled with gloves, folded leaves, or thick cloth.
*   **Dietary Context:** Greens alone cannot sustain survival; they supplement a diet based on roots, nuts, and animal protein.

## Material
*   Dandelion (*Taraxacum officinale*)
*   Stinging Nettle (*Urtica dioica*)
*   Broadleaf Plantain (*Plantago major*)

## System
*   **Environment:** Temperate zones, disturbed soil (near ruins, roads, fields, camps, footpaths, driveways, lawns, campsite edges). 
*   **Soil Preference:** Moist, nitrogen-rich soil, indicating productive foraging areas.

## Technology
*   **Identification:** Visual confirmation of leaves, stems, flowers, and roots; recognizing distinct features to avoid dangerous look-alikes.

## Tools
*   Digging stick or knife (for root harvest).
*   Container (for collecting seeds).
*   Gloves, thick cloth, or firm pinching technique (for handling nettles).
*   Pot and water (for boiling nettles and preparing roots/leaves).
