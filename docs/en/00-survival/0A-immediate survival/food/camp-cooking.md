# Camp Cooking

## Objective
To safely cook food using simple containers, hot coals and improvised cooking structures.

## Dependencies and prerequisites
- Safe fire.
- Suitable cooking area.
- Heat-resistant container or cooking surface.
- Reliable fuel supply.

## Material
- Metal pot or suitable heat-resistant container.
- Pan/skillet where available.
- Green hardwood sticks.
- Flat stones where appropriate.
- Cordage.
- Clean water.
- Food-safe utensils.

## System
- Cooking should use controlled heat rather than simply the largest possible flame.
- Coals provide more controllable heat than an open flame for many cooking tasks.
- Cooking structures should be stable and positioned so that people do not have to reach through flames.

## Technology
- Cooking over coals.
- Pot suspension.
- Improvised grill.
- Rotisserie.
- Hot-stone cooking.
- Keyhole fire cooking zone.

## Tools
- Pot.
- Pan.
- Cooking sticks.
- Pot hanger.
- Grill or improvised support.

## Process
1. Establish a stable fire.
2. Allow a suitable coal bed to develop.
3. Select the cooking method according to the food.
4. Keep containers and utensils clean.
5. Use green hardwood for improvised cooking sticks.
6. Avoid resinous wood when making direct-contact cooking supports.
7. Cook food thoroughly when safety requires it.
8. Preserve unused food promptly.

## Notes
- The source specifically recommends green hardwood rather than resinous wood for improvised rotisserie/- cooking sticks.
- Cooking should be considered part of the fire-management system, not a separate activity.
