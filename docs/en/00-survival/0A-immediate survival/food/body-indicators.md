# Body Indicators | Survival Wiki

## Objective
> Part of Weather Forecasting Using your own body’s physical responses to detect pressure and humidity changes before storms arrive.

## Dependencies and prerequisites
*   A willingness to observe and pay attention to bodily responses.
*   A multi-indicator system to confirm readings.

## Material
> Your own body’s physical responses to detect pressure and humidity changes before storms arrive.

## System
> Body Indicators: A system where physical responses are used as early warnings for approaching weather changes.

## Technology
*   **Barometric Pressure:** Drops in atmospheric pressure cause soft tissue to swell slightly, putting pressure on nerves.
*   **Hygroscopy:** Human hair absorbs moisture from the air, changing length in response to relative humidity.

## Tools
*   Personal log (to track body symptoms and following weather).
*   Multi-indicator system (to confirm readings).

## Process
1.  **Build a Personal Sensitivity Map:** Over several weeks of observation, log body symptoms alongside the weather that followed to discover reliable indicators and lead times.
2.  **Observe Indicators:** Pay attention to specific symptoms:
    *   **Joint and Injury Pain:** Indicates pressure changes affecting enclosed joint capsules.
    *   **Ear Pressure:** Indicates rapid pressure changes outpacing the Eustachian tube's ability to equalize.
    *   **Headaches:** Indicates pressure change affecting blood vessels in the brain.
    *   **Hair and Skin:** Indicates rising humidity.
    *   **Breathing and Energy:** Subtle signs of slightly less oxygen due to low pressure.
3.  **Use as Early Warning:** Body indicators serve as an early warning trigger, prompting closer observation using other methods (like checking smoke behavior or listening for sounds).

## Notes
*   Body indicators should never be relied upon alone, as they can be triggered by exertion, illness, dehydration, or altitude changes unrelated to weather.
*   The reliability and lead time vary significantly between individuals; some may get 12+ hours from joint pain, while others get only 2-3 hours from ear pressure.
*   The connection between body aches and weather is scientifically confirmed, stemming from barometric pressure causing tissue swelling.
*   Weather headaches are distinguished by feeling like pressure rather than sharp pain and often correlating with other pressure indicators.
