# Boiling Purification

## Objective
To make water safe to drink.

## Dependencies and prerequisites
* Fire (to generate heat)
* Water
* Containers (fireproof or otherwise)
* Dense, dry stones (for the Hot Stone Method)
* Specific rocks (igneous rocks or dense river stones for Hot Stone Method)
* Bamboo culm (for Bamboo Boiling)
* Beeswax or soy wax (for the Wax Indicator Trick)

## Material
* Water
* Pathogens (bacteria, viruses, protozoan cysts, parasitic worms)
* Stones (igneous rocks, basalt, dense river stones)
* Charcoal
* Cloth
* Wax or soy wax

## System
**Multi-barrier Approach:** The gold standard for improvised water purification, involving sequential treatment steps to address different contaminants.

## Technology
**Heat-Based Disinfection:** Heat kills living organisms by denaturing their proteins, which stops enzyme function, causes cell membrane rupture, and destroys DNA integrity.

**Pathogen Thermal Thresholds (Killed at/Time Required):**
| Pathogen | Killed At | Time Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| E. coli | 70 C (158 F) | Instant |
| Salmonella | 60 C (140 F) | 5-10 minutes |
| Vibrio cholerae (cholera) | 70 C (158 F) | Instant |
| Hepatitis A virus | 85 C (185 F) | 1 minute |
| Rotavirus | 70 C (158 F) | Instant |
| Giardia cysts | 60 C (140 F) | 10 minutes |
| Cryptosporidium oocysts | 72 C (162 F) | 1 minute |
| Parasitic worm eggs | 65 C (149 F) | Several minutes |

**Key Insight:** The rolling boil (100 C at sea level) ensures that every pathogen listed above is dead.

## Tools
* Wooden tongs
* Two sticks or a forked branch (for transferring hot stones)
* Narrow-bottomed vessel (for fuel efficiency)
* Lids (for fuel efficiency)

## Process
**1. The Rolling Boil (Primary Method)**
*   Bring water to a vigorous, rolling boil.
*   Maintain the boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 2,000 meters elevation).

**2. Pasteurization (Lower Temperature, Longer Time)**
*   Heat water to 65-75 C (149-167 F).
*   Maintain for at least 6 minutes.
*   Cool and cover.
*   *Wax Indicator Trick:* Wax melts at 62-65 C; melting and floating indicates pasteurization temperature.

**3. The Hot Stone Method**
*   Select dense, dry igneous rocks (granite, basalt) or dense river stones. Avoid porous or wet stones.
*   Heat stones in coals for 30-45 minutes until glowing.
*   Transfer hot stones one at a time into the water container.
*   Maintain the boil by swapping in fresh stones until a rolling boil is achieved.
*   Maintain the boil for at least 1 minute.
*   Remove stones and allow water to cool.

**4. Bamboo Boiling**
*   Cut a large-diameter bamboo culm (8-12 cm diameter) with a node at the bottom to create a sealed cup.
*   Fill the tube with water, leaving 5 cm of air space at the top.
*   Prop the tube over the fire.
*   The water prevents the inner wall temperature from exceeding 100 C.

**5. Multi-barrier Approach (Comprehensive Treatment)**
1. Settle: Let turbid water sit for 1-2 hours for sediment to sink.
2. Pre-filter: Pour through cloth to remove visible particles.
3. Charcoal filter: Pass through charcoal to remove chemicals and improve taste.
4. Boil: Bring to a rolling boil for 1 minute to kill all pathogens.
5. Store safely: Keep in a covered, clean container.

## Notes
*   **Limitations of Boiling:** Boiling is ineffective against chemical contamination (heavy metals, pesticides), radioactive contamination, turbidity (sediment), and salt (seawater). Distillation is required for desalination and chemical removal.
*   **Fuel Efficiency Techniques:** Using lids (20-30% less fuel), minimizing water volume, shielding from wind (30-40% less fuel), using narrow-bottomed vessels (10-15% less fuel), and using pasteurization (30-50% less fuel).
*   **Fuel Efficiency System:** A well-designed rocket stove can use 60-70% less fuel than an open fire.
