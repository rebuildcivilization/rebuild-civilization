# 00 - Survival

This phase covers immediate survival and the management of the first emergencies, from the onset of a crisis through the first week or month.

## How to Use This Phase

The time periods used in this phase are organizational guidelines, not fixed survival deadlines.

Immediate priorities depend on the actual situation, including injuries, environmental exposure, available resources, and the likelihood of rescue.

Treat life-threatening conditions first. Then address immediate hazards, shelter, water, communication, and other essential needs according to local conditions.

The techniques described here are not substitutes for professional training, medical care, or official emergency instructions.


## 0A - Immediate survival

Actions focused on immediate life-safety priorities during the first 72 hours.


## 0B - Emergency stabilization

Actions for managing hazards, maintaining essential needs, and organizing a response during the first week to month.
