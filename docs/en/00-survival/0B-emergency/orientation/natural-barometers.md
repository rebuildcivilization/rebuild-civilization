# Natural Barometers

## Objective
Part of Weather Forecasting using smoke, sound, and smell as natural instruments to detect pressure changes and predict weather.

## Dependencies and prerequisites
- Ability to light a fire (for smoke indicator).
- A known distance landmark sound source (for sound propagation testing).
- An established baseline for natural odors for smell intensity testing.

## Material
- Smoke (hot air carrying particles).
- Sound (travel distance and volume).
- Smells (volatile compounds released from soil, water, and organic matter).
- Animal behavior (response of birds, insects, frogs, and toads).

## System
The system involves combining a minimum of three natural indicators using a scoring system to determine the likelihood of weather events.

## Technology
- Physics of Smoke:
    - High pressure: Smoke rises in a straight, tall column.
    - Falling pressure: Smoke rises weakly, swirls, and spreads horizontally.
    - Low pressure: Smoke flattens immediately, hugs the ground, and spreads outward.
- Physics of Sound:
    - Pressure drops change the air density gradient, causing sound waves to refract downward, making distant sounds audible.
- Physics of Smell:
    - Falling pressure releases trapped gases, allowing volatile compounds to escape more freely.

## Tools
- Campfire (for smoke observation).
- Sound detection (to test propagation).
- Olfactory senses (to detect key smells like Petrichor or Ozone).
- Observation of Animal Behavior (birds, bees, frogs, toads).

## Process
1. **Establish Baseline:** Establish a baseline for natural odors and normal sound levels.
2. **Observation:** Monitor indicators (smoke behavior, sound changes, smell intensity, animal actions).
3. **Correlation:** Correlate multiple indicators before acting on a prediction.
4. **Scoring:** Assign points based on observed indicators (e.g., Smoke flattening = +2; Bees returning to hive = +2).
5. **Decision Making:**
    - Score 4+ indicates rain likely within 12 hours. Action: Secure shelter, gather firewood, protect supplies.
    - Score 6+ indicates a storm probable within 6-8 hours. Action: Postpone travel, reinforce shelter.

Notes
- Natural barometers primarily indicate pressure trends, not exact values.
- Limitations include the fact that wind can distort smoke readings, urban noise can mask sound propagation changes, and seasonal variation in animal behavior can be misleading.
- Developing consistent observation over a month leads to surprisingly reliable weather intuition.
- Key indicators for falling pressure include Petrichor, stronger swamp/marsh odors, stronger compost/manure, stronger flowers, and Ozone.
