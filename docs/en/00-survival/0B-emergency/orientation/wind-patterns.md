# Wind Patterns | Survival Wiki

## Objective
To predict approaching weather systems and frontal passages using wind direction shifts and patterns.

## Dependencies and prerequisites
Mastery of the relationship between wind direction and pressure systems, and the ability to track wind direction at dawn, midday, and dusk.

## Material
Wind direction and patterns, pressure systems (high and low pressure areas), and weather fronts (cold, warm, and occluded).

## System
The flow of wind from high-pressure areas to low-pressure areas, deflected by Earth’s rotation (the Coriolis effect).

## Technology
*   **Buys Ballot’s Law:** The core rule for wind-based forecasting.
*   **Wind Classification:** Differentiation between Veering winds and Backing winds.
*   **Frontal Analysis:** Identification of wind characteristics associated with cold fronts, warm fronts, and occluded fronts.
*   **Local Wind Patterns:** Recognition of sea/lake breezes, valley winds (anabatic/katabatic), and mountain gap winds.

## Tools
*   Observation of wind direction.
*   Observation of wind speed (relative to the Beaufort scale, though instruments are not required).
*   Observation of temperature changes.

## Process
1.  **Determine Pressure Relationship:** Use wind direction to determine the relative position of high/low pressure systems.
2.  **Apply Buys Ballot’s Law:** Use the rule for Northern/Southern Hemisphere to predict the immediate pressure area.
3.  **Analyze Wind Shifts:** Identify the type of front by observing the wind shift at the frontal passage (e.g., clockwise shift for a cold front).
4.  **Determine Weather Trend:** Use Veering/Backing wind analysis to determine if conditions are improving or deteriorating.
5.  **Integrate Local Factors:** Check for sea/lake breeze failure or valley wind patterns to confirm large-scale system approaches.
6.  **Assess Intensity:** Determine if wind speed increase/decrease indicates the driving system is strengthening or weakening.

Notes
- In the Northern Hemisphere: Veering winds generally indicate improving weather; Backing winds indicate deteriorating weather.
- In the Southern Hemisphere: The associations for Veering and Backing winds are reversed.
- A sudden calm after strong winds is considered dangerous and may indicate being in the center of a low-pressure system or storm eye.
- Practical Forecasting Rules include: South winds bringing warmth followed by a clockwise shift to west = cold front passage; Wind increasing overnight = large weather system approaching.
