# Signal Mirror

## Objective

To attract the attention of rescuers using reflected sunlight.

## Dependencies and prerequisites

A signal mirror works best when:

* There is direct sunlight.
* The target is visible or approximately located.
* The mirror can be aimed accurately.
* The user remains in a location where rescuers can observe the signal.

## Material

* Signaling mirror.
* Reflective material when a dedicated mirror is unavailable.
  -  polished metal;
  -  glass;
  -  chrome;
  -  other bright reflective surfaces.
* Open or elevated location.

## System

The objective is to create a bright, moving reflection that attracts attention.

A short, controlled flash is generally more noticeable than simply holding a mirror toward the sun.

## Technology

A mirror redirects sunlight into a concentrated reflected beam.

Small changes in mirror orientation can move the reflected light over a large area.

## Tools

* Signaling mirror.
* Polished reflective surface.
* Other suitable reflective material.

## Process

1. Move to a location visible from the air or from the expected direction of rescuers.
2. Face the approximate direction of the target.
3. Use the mirror to locate the reflected sunlight.
4. Aim the reflection toward the target.
5. Move the reflected spot deliberately.
6. Repeat the signal periodically.
7. Continue signaling while conserving energy.

## Notes

* A signaling mirror is particularly useful when fire or smoke is impractical.
* The repository should treat the mirror as a signaling technology rather than as a specific military item.
* In a post-disaster environment, reflective salvage can replace a dedicated signal mirror.
