# Signaling

## Objective
To attract the attention of rescuers.

## Dependencies and prerequisites
The process depends on whether it is night or day.

## Material

### Day

- mirror;
- whistle;
- smoke;
- brightly colored material.

### Night

- light;
- strobe;
- chemlight;
- fire;
- whistle.

## System

The objective is to attracts attention.

## Tools


## Process

signaling should begin early when rescue is expected; do not wait until all other resources are exhausted.
