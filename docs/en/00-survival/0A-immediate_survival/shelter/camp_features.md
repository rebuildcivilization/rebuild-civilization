## System
* Toilet areas must be located away from the camp and water sources.
* The area used for collecting water must be treated as a zone protected from contamination.
* Good camp hygiene is an integral part of ensuring water safety.

## Process
- Set aside an area for organic waste away from water.
- Keep the toilet area separate from where water is collected and prepared.
- For short stays, use a suitable pit latrine and cover it.
- For longer stays, organise a proper toilet facility for the group.
- Wash your hands after using the toilet and before preparing food.

## Notes
- It is not enough simply to purify the water; we must prevent the camp from contaminating it again
