# Venomous Bites and Stings

## Objective

To recognize potentially dangerous bites and stings, reduce additional exposure, and obtain medical assistance as soon as possible.

## Dependencies and prerequisites

Risk depends strongly on:

* Species.
* Geographic region.
* Amount of venom delivered.
* Location of the bite or sting.
* Victim's condition.
* Time to medical care.

Identification should never be based solely on superficial appearance when it is uncertain.

## Material

Useful materials include:

* Clean dressings.
* First-aid supplies.
* Gloves.
* Communication equipment.
* Container or camera for safe documentation of the animal when possible.

## System

### Avoid Further Exposure

Move away from the animal or hazard.

Do not attempt to capture or kill a venomous animal for identification.

### Observation

Record, when safely possible:

* Time of exposure.
* Location.
* Circumstances.
* Symptoms.
* Description of the animal.

### Monitoring

Monitor the victim for worsening symptoms while arranging medical assistance.

## Technology

Identification information for:

* Venomous snakes.
* Dangerous lizards.
* Dangerous fish.
* Jellyfish.
* Blue-ringed octopus.
* Cone shells.
* Auger shells.

The most useful repository content is the recognition of hazardous groups and the principle of avoiding contact.

## Tools

* First-aid kit.
* Clean dressings.
* Gloves.
* Communication device.

## Process

1. Move away from the animal.
2. Keep the victim calm and as still as practical.
3. Arrange emergency medical assistance.
4. Record the time of exposure.
5. Monitor breathing, consciousness, and progression of symptoms.
6. Follow current medical guidance for the specific bite or sting.
7. Do not improvise invasive treatment based on old survival manuals.

## Notes

* Some marine animals remain dangerous after death or when apparently stranded.
* Warns about jellyfish tentacles, blue-ringed octopus, cone shells, and auger shells.
* Specific treatment procedures should come from current medical authorities rather than this document.
